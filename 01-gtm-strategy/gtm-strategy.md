## 2. The problem & the audience

- **Problem worth solving:** The acute financial anxiety of fronting thousands of dollars on a personal credit card, compounded by the administrative nightmare of chasing friends for Venmo repayments and juggling chaotic group chat debates over what to actually do.
- **Primary ICP:** The Milestone Trip Organizer who holds the buying authority but bears all the financial and logistical risk.
- **What they do today (the alternative):** A fragmented manual stack (Google Sheets + chaotic WhatsApp chats for planning + personal credit cards + Venmo/Splitwise for payments) or hyper-niche party tools like The Bach App.

## 3. GTM motion

**Chosen motion:** Pure Product-Led Growth (PLG)

_Why is this the right motion for this audience and price point?_

Time-to-value must be measured in minutes and the sales cycle must be entirely self-serve. At a low-to-mid ACV, we cannot afford human sales intervention. Most importantly, the feature scales via inherent product virality.

- **Acquisition:** Driven by the Organizer sending a secure split-payment and itinerary link to Invitees, bypassing traditional top-of-funnel marketing costs.
- **Activation:** The Organizer builds the foundation of the trip; Invitees activate by creating a Roamly account to pay their exact share within a 24-hour hold window and gain access to the collaborative, centralized itinerary.
- **Monetization:** Transactional take-rate on the booked inventory (seats secured).

## 6. The assumption I'm most worried about

_Pick the one assumption that, if wrong, breaks the strategy — and how you'd test it._

**The Assumption:** Our supply-side inventory can actually handle large groups. Roamly’s 12,000 hosts built their businesses around independent travelers. If a local artisan cannot physically host 10 people, the PLG loop breaks at the point of fulfillment.

**How to test it:** Before launch, run an operational sprint to categorize and explicitly label our existing supply base for capacity limits and occasion suitability (e.g., "Verified for 10+"). Only expose group-verified hosts to the Roamly Groups booking flow.
