01 · GTM Strategy & Engine

Core GTM Motion

Pure Product-Led Growth (PLG)

  • Acquisition: Organizer distributes split-payment & itinerary link, acquiring Invitees at near-zero CAC.
  • Activation: Invitees onboard by paying their exact share within a 24-hr hold window to unlock the collaborative hub.
  • Monetization: Transactional take-rate captured on completed inventory bookings.
  • Self-Serve Velocity: Low-to-mid ACV requires pure self-serve execution measured in minutes.
Target Segment & ICP

The Milestone Trip Organizer

  • Profile: Friend-group ringleader coordinating 30th/40th birthdays, bachelor/ettes, or reunions.
  • Acute Problem: Financial exposure of fronting thousands on personal cards, chasing Venmo payments, and handling messy group chat plans.

The Discover Framework

Demand: High pull via manual split requests & cart drops.
Offer: Zero-risk hold + shared planning.
Validation: Drop-off spike on 4+ ticket carts ($1.5k+).

Leading Indicator: Invitee Capture Rate (K-Factor viral coefficient).

Core Vulnerability

Assumption: 12k hosts can handle large groups.
Mitigation: Pre-launch sprint to tag inventory as "Verified for 10+" before exposure.

05 · Pricing & Packaging Recommendation

Model & Goal

Transaction-Based Model

  • Commercial Goal: Maximize revenue from a known audience by recovering stranded multi-ticket cart GMV.
  • Flexible Payment: Fractional split across individual links OR single-payer cover-all option.
  • V1 Free Packaging: Group tools 100% free to maximize checkout completion and PLG invitee loops.
Research Validation

Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity

  • Study Target: Survey historical multi-ticket cart abandoners across 4 pricing perception questions.
  • Assumption to Validate: The exact acceptable transaction fee % before Invitees deem it "too expensive."
  • Fatal Risk: Invitee fee sensitivity. If the fee is too high, invitees revert to Venmo, cart drops below 100%, and the booking dies.

06 · Individual Insights & Synthesis

Friction Points

Monetization vs. Growth

Deciding whether to monetize software capabilities was the hardest challenge. Leaning toward a SaaS fee broke the PLG viral loop.

Solution: Treated Groups as an unblocking mechanism—keeping the toolset free captures high-basket GMV while preserving invitee account acquisition.

The "Aha" Moment

The Invitee Engine

The Invitee is not just a participant settling a bill—they are the primary customer acquisition engine.

Realization: The invitee checkout is a zero-CAC Trojan Horse. Any pricing or UX friction placed on the invitee kills the viral loop before activation.

Key Takeaways

Strategic Principles

  • Value Alignment: Monetize when value is experienced (completed bookings), not arbitrarily.
  • Ungated Virality: Packaging by features only scales if viral hooks remain completely accessible.
  • Empirical Pricing: Van Westendorp research prevents killing conversion at checkout.
GTM Summary

Roamly Groups turns group travel coordination from an administrative failure point into a viral acquisition and high-margin revenue engine.
