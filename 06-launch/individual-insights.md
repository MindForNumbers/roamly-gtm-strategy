## 1. Launch plan

| Phase | Goal | Key activities | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pre-launch** | Supply readiness & price validation | Categorize 12k hosts with "Verified for 10+" tags; execute Van Westendorp pricing survey on churned organizers. | Product & Host Ops |
| **Launch** | Stop checkout bleeding | Roll out ungated Roamly Groups to 4+ ticket carts; activate in-app interception and predictive lifecycle emails. | Product Marketing |
| **Post-launch** | Optimize viral acquisition loop | Measure Invitee K-Factor; refine post-trip onboarding flows to convert new Invitees into solo bookers. | Growth & Data |

## 2. Launch metrics & guardrails

- **Success looks like:** A >20% increase in cart completion rates for multi-ticket carts (4+ people) compared to our historical single-payer baseline, alongside a high Invitee Capture Rate (K-Factor).
- **Tripwire (when to pull back):** Invitee cart abandonment spikes due to take-rate fee sensitivity. If Invitees defect to Venmo, the group misses 100% funding, the booking collapses, and we must immediately pull back and adjust the pricing threshold.

## 3. The full GTM story in one slide

_How do the six deliverables connect into one coherent bet for Roamly Groups?_

Roamly Groups turns group travel coordination from an administrative failure point into a viral acquisition and high-margin revenue engine. By keeping the native split-payment and shared itinerary features ungated for the Organizer, we instantly recover stranded multi-ticket GMV while using the Invitee checkout flow as a zero-CAC Trojan Horse to acquire entire networks of net-new users.

## 4. Individual insights

_This is the reflection you share. Be honest and specific._

- **Biggest "aha" across the certification:** The Invitee is not just a secondary participant settling a bill—they are the primary customer acquisition engine. Realizing the invitee checkout is our core growth loop completely shifted how I approached pricing friction.
- **What changed in how you think about GTM:** I realized monetization must align with how customers experience value, not how product teams want to extract it. Defaulting to a SaaS subscription would have completely broken the PLG motion.
- **What you'd do differently with more time / data:** I would want empirical operational data on our supply side to see exactly how many of our 12,000 local hosts can physically accommodate groups of 10+ without degrading the cultural experience.
- **The one idea you'll apply at work on Monday:** I'm bringing the "From/To" market shift messaging framework directly into my campaign planning at KeyPoint Credit Union to ensure our next member promotion focuses on the emotional relief of the end-state rather than just listing financial features.
