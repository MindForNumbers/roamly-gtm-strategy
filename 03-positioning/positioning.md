## 2. Core product truth

*The one thing that is undeniably true about Roamly Groups that competitors can't easily claim.*

We control both the vetted supply and the financial plumbing. Tools like Splitwise or Venmo manage the math but have no bookable inventory; platforms like Airbnb Experiences have inventory but force single-payer friction. Roamly Groups connects native split-payments and itinerary planning directly to guaranteed, capacity-verified local hosts.

## 3. Positioning statement

*For [target customer] who [need], Roamly Groups is the [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [alternative], Roamly Groups [differentiator].*

For **Milestone Trip Organizers** who **need to coordinate multi-player travel**, Roamly Groups is the **native split-payment and shared itinerary planning engine** that **locks in group availability instantly without forcing one person to front the bill or manage a massive spreadsheet**. Unlike **the manual Venmo/WhatsApp status quo**, Roamly Groups **offers premium, culturally vetted local experiences with a frictionless group checkout and a centralized collaborative hub**.

## 4. Pressure tests

*Run the statement through each test and note what you'd fix.*

| Test | Pass? | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ownable** — could a competitor say the same? | Yes | Standard OTAs cannot claim the "frictionless group checkout" or "cultural vetting" without overhauling their payment architecture and host onboarding. |
| **Believable** — is the proof real? | Yes | The proof relies on our existing 12,000-host marketplace; the believability hinges entirely on our ability to accurately tag and enforce host capacity limits. |
| **Relevant** — does the ICP care? | Yes | It directly targets the highest emotional, financial, and logistical friction points of the Organizer: acting as the unpaid bank and spreadsheet manager. |
| **Durable** — holds as the product grows? | Yes | As long as we aggressively enforce supply-side capacity quality as we scale. If hosts cannot honor 10+ person bookings, the positioning falls apart. |

## 5. Positioning vs. messaging note

*How does this fixed positioning translate to the homepage headline vs. the sales opener vs. the app store blurb?*

*   **Homepage Headline (Top of Funnel):** Translates the positioning into an emotional hook focusing on the end state (e.g., "Book the perfect group trip without playing bank for your friends").
*   **Host Onboarding/Sales Opener (Supply-Side):** Flips the positioning to focus on the operational relief and guaranteed revenue (e.g., "Turn chaotic group inquiries into guaranteed, fully paid bookings without partial cancellation risk").
*   **App Store Blurb (Feature Level):** Translates the positioning into literal, mechanical capabilities (e.g., "Split payments instantly, build shared itineraries, and book verified local hosts").
