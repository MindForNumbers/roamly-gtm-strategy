## 1. Fro/To market shift

*What changed in the world that made Roamly Groups necessary? A shift in the world, not a feature.*

| From (life before) | To (life after) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Booking a group trip meant one person had to float thousands of dollars on a personal credit card and spend weeks playing debt collector and camp counselor across chaotic group chats. | The group secures the trip instantly, the financial burden is entirely decentralized, and the entire trip is managed in a single, collaborative itinerary hub. |

## 2. Audience segmentation

*There's no single right number — make a defensible call and justify it.*

*   **Number of audiences that need their own pillar:** 3
*   **Justification — why each is distinct enough to need its own message:** The Organizer, the Invitee, and the Host represent three distinct stakeholders with entirely different emotional stakes. The Organizer's pain is financial exposure and administrative planning anxiety. The Invitee's pain is logistical hassle; they need a frictionless zero-CAC checkout and easy access to the trip plan. The Host is the supply-side fulfiller; they want high revenue but fear the operational risk of flakes and partial payments. One message cannot carry all three.

## 3. Messaging pillars

### Pillar — Audience 1: The Milestone Trip Organizer

| Layer | Message |
| :--- | :--- |
| **🎯 Value prop** | Lock in the perfect dates for your group immediately, without ever acting as the unpaid bank or the nagging camp counselor. You get to be a traveler, not an accountant or spreadsheet manager. |
| **🏆 Capabilities** | Instant reservation holds with zero upfront personal charges. Native split-payment links that distribute exact costs to each friend. A centralized tracking dashboard and shared itinerary hub to monitor payments and build the trip, entirely eliminating Google Sheets. |
| **📊 Evidence** | 12,000 vetted local experts across 40 cities with categorical tagging for group size. Historical data tying multi-ticket cart abandonment to single-payer friction. |

### Pillar — Audience 2: The Group Invitee 

| Layer | Message |
| :--- | :--- |
| **🎯 Value prop** | Pay your exact, fair share in under 30 seconds and instantly access the group's shared itinerary so you can focus on the trip, not awkward Venmo reminders or lost text threads. |
| **🏆 Capabilities** | A frictionless payment link that functions as an interactive product demo without requiring a heavy app download. Immediate post-payment access to the centralized, collaborative itinerary hub. |
| **📊 Evidence** | Universal consumer fatigue surrounding group trip debt, peer-to-peer payment chasing, and fragmented group chat planning. |

### Pillar — Audience 3: The Host (Supply-Side)

| Layer | Message |
| :--- | :--- |
| **🎯 Value prop** | Maximize your revenue per timeslot with high-value group bookings, without the risk of unpaid no-shows, partial cancellations, or exceeding your physical capacity. |
| **🏆 Capabilities** | Upfront capacity labeling (e.g., "Verified for 10+") matching your operational limits. Native split-payments ensure the booking is financially locked in by the entire group before they arrive. |
| **📊 Evidence** | Successful categorization of 12,000 vetted local experts by exact group capacity, removing guesswork and mitigating supply-side risk. |
