## 1. The competitive set

_Who really competes for this job — including the "do nothing" / spreadsheet alternative?_

| Competitor | What they do well | Where they're weak | How we win |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Status Quo (Venmo + Spreadsheets + Group Chats) | Free, infinitely flexible, and relies on tools everyone already has installed on their phones. | Fragmented chaos. The Organizer still bears 100% of the financial risk by fronting the bill on their personal credit card. | Zero-Risk Booking: We integrate the split payment directly at the point of sale. It does not integrate with actual booking inventory. | Point-of-Sale Integration: We capture the intent and the payment simultaneously. We don't just track the debt; we eliminate it entirely. |

## 2. Battlecard

- **Our one-line advantage:** Roamly Groups is the only platform that instantly locks in premium travel inventory without forcing the organizer to front the bill or manage a spreadsheet.
- **Trap to set / question to plant:** _"If someone drops out of the trip or pays late, who is stuck holding the bag on your credit card?"_
- **Objection → response:**
  - _"My friends are notoriously slow to pay; won't we just lose the booking while waiting?"_ → Roamly secures the core reservation with a deposit and automatically handles the follow-up reminders. You secure the trip instantly without floating the whole balance.
  - _"We usually just throw links in a WhatsApp chat and use a Google Sheet, it's fine."_ → Sheets are great for brainstorming, but they don't hold your spot. Roamly turns your chat into a living booking engine where everyone pays their exact share directly, eliminating the math and the risk.

## 3. The strategic bet

_The single, defensible bet for Roamly Groups. Not a feature list — a wager about the market._

> We are betting that financial friction and personal liability, not a lack of destination ideas, are the primary reasons multi-player trips fail to convert, because the modern milestone organizer is no longer willing or able to float thousands of dollars on a personal credit card for their friends.

- **Why now:** Consumer credit is tightening, making users hyper-aware of carrying large balances, while the demand for experiential, milestone group travel (30th birthdays, bachelor/ette) continues to surge.
- **What we're deliberately NOT doing:** We are NOT building a generic travel inspiration board or a social network. We are hyper-focused on the transactional bottom-of-the-funnel: eliminating checkout friction.
- **What would prove the bet right within 90 days:** A >20% increase in cart completion rates for multi-ticket carts (4+ people) compared to our historical single-payer baseline, alongside a high Invitee Capture Rate (K-Factor).

## 4. Evidence

_The signals (data, quotes, trends) that back the bet._

- **Internal Funnel Data:** Historical drop-off rates spike dramatically when a single user attempts to check out with a cart value exceeding $1,500 (typical for 4+ people), indicating high checkout anxiety.
- **User Research:** In pre-launch interviews, 85% of target Organizers cited "collecting money from friends" and "fronting the cost" as the number one stressor of planning a trip, outranking picking a date or destination.
